# Writing the words

Copy is in scope. The client expects headlines and card copy **reconsidered and improved**,
not poured into boxes verbatim. A brief is raw material.

## Voice

Warm, plain, clinical without being cold. She is a psychiatrist speaking to families, not
a wellness account. Second person where it helps ("check in tomorrow, and the day after"),
never a lecture.

- **The occasion outranks the slogan.** The title is the day or the topic. A mantra is
  support, and there is at most one per slide.
- **Card copy: one sentence, ~12–16 words.** If it runs longer, cut the sentence — never
  the font size.
- **One idea per card, one idea per slide.** Three cards are three beats of one thought,
  not three unrelated tips.
- Imperatives make good card headings: `ASK` · `LISTEN` · `STAY`. Single words, 27px,
  3.4px tracking, uppercase.
- The closing line is the only place a Caveat script line appears on a message slide. Keep
  it under about eight words.
- Spell out contractions sparingly; use the typographic apostrophe (`&rsquo;`).
- No emoji, no hashtags in the artwork. Those live in the caption.

## Safe messaging — requirements, not taste

On suicide, self-harm, overdose or addiction topics, these are not stylistic preferences:

- **Never depict or imply a method**, in words or imagery.
- **No imagery of visible anguish.** The imagery holds space; it does not illustrate pain.
- **"died by suicide"**, never "committed suicide". Not "successful"/"failed attempt".
- **Attribute any statistic on screen**, with the source and year. Do not put an
  unverified figure under her name.
- **A crisis resource belongs on the post**, and **before** any clinic promotion when both
  appear. If no verified local helpline number is available, say so to the user and let
  them decide — do not invent one and do not quietly ship without one.
- Naming suicide directly does not plant the idea; saying so plainly is good practice and
  is on-brand for her.

## Contact data — always exactly these

```
MON-SAT | 6:00 – 9:00 PM
0315-7090609
MBBS, MD, MRCPsych(UK)
@dr.monaalisardar
Psychiatric & Drug Rehabilitation Clinic
```

**Never use a placeholder.** Briefs that supply `dr-mona.com`, `1-800-…` or a generic
address are ignored in favour of these.

## Caption

When asked for a caption alongside the artwork, keep the same voice, put the crisis
resource in the first three lines on a safe-messaging topic, and end with the booking
details and `@dr.monaalisardar`. Hashtags go on the last line, ten at most.
